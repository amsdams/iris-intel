IRIS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

IRIS is an independent, open-source Ingress Intel companion maintained by Julien Langeveld (IRIS project). It is not affiliated with Niantic Spatial or Google. This policy describes the IRIS Android app.

Intel and authentication

The app loads the Ingress Intel website and provides the IRIS interface within Android's WebView. Signing in contacts Niantic Spatial and the authentication provider you choose. Intel requests use your authenticated session to retrieve map, agent, portal, COMM, mission and inventory information, and to perform actions you request, such as sending COMM messages or redeeming a passcode. Inventory access depends on your account's subscription and service availability.

The WebView stores session cookies and website data on your device. IRIS does not operate an authentication server or receive copies of your credentials on a maintainer-operated backend. The current login uses an experimental embedded-browser compatibility workaround. Your authentication provider and Niantic Spatial handle information according to their own policies.

Maps, searches and images

Your selected base map contacts OpenStreetMap or OpenFreeMap and the resources used by that provider. Address searches send the entered query to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Portal, mission and map-decoration images load from their supplied hosts, including Google-hosted Ingress resources. These services receive network information such as your IP address and the requested map area, search query or image identifier. An automatic base-map fallback can contact another supported provider if the selected map fails.

Optional location and clipboard

Locate requests Android foreground location permission when you choose that feature. Approximate or precise location is used to center the map. Subsequent Intel and base-map requests can reveal that area to their providers. IRIS does not request background location permission. You can deny or revoke permission in Android settings and continue using the other features.

Copy actions place the data you select, such as a portal title, drawn markers or diagnostics, on your device clipboard. Pasting or importing text makes the text available to the feature you use. Android and apps you subsequently paste into control further clipboard handling.

Updates, feedback and diagnostics

The GitHub APK's manual update check requests public build metadata from GitHub, without attaching Intel cookies or account details. The Play build opens its Google Play listing for updates and does not use that GitHub update check. The chosen service receives the network request. Feedback opens GitHub Issues in your browser. External browser links are also subject to that browser's session and privacy settings.

IRIS has no maintainer-operated analytics or automatic crash-reporting service. Diagnostics are shared only if you copy and send them. They contain app, Android and WebView versions, hosts and fixed status messages; they exclude cookies, tokens, player identity and response bodies. Screenshots or reports you add may contain personal or game information. Review them before sharing. GitHub Issues are public: never submit passwords, cookies, tokens or private portal information.

Storage, retention and deletion

Session data, website storage and IRIS preferences are stored locally. Settings, map view and saved drawing data remain until you change or clear them or remove the app's storage. Some retrieved game data is cached in memory while the app is running. Android backups are disabled for this app. IRIS does not maintain a separate server-side user profile.

Intel logout signs you out while preserving preferences. To remove all app-local session and settings data, use Android Settings → Apps → IRIS → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall IRIS. Clearing app data does not remove text already copied elsewhere, files you saved separately or reports submitted to GitHub.

Clearing IRIS is not deletion of your Ingress, Google or other provider account, COMM messages, or data those providers retain. Use the relevant provider's account and deletion controls. Feedback remains with its recipient under that service's retention policy; contact the maintainer if you need help removing information from a project issue. The maintainer cannot delete data held independently by those providers.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the IRIS issue tracker without including sensitive information. Ask for a private contact route if needed. This policy will be updated when the app's data handling changes; the date above identifies this version.
