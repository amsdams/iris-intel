Privacy policy

IRIS for Ingress (also called IRIS)
Android package: io.github.amsdams.iris

Effective and last updated: 29 October 2026

Who provides IRIS

IRIS is an independent, open-source Intel map companion maintained by the IRIS project maintainer, amsdams. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Niantic, Google, or IITC. For support, privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to the maintainer, email 1sos.2play@gmail.com.

This policy covers the IRIS Android app, its related browser extensions and this privacy webpage. The app uses Intel, authentication, map and hosting services operated by others. Their own policies also apply when you use those services.

What the app accesses and why

  • Sign-in and session information. The Android app loads Intel and its sign-in providers in a WebView. Login information is submitted to those services, and the WebView retains session cookies and website storage so you can remain signed in. IRIS uses that session to make Intel requests for the features you use. There is no separate IRIS account or IRIS-operated account server.
  • Ingress account and game data. IRIS requests, processes and displays data available through your Intel session, such as agent profile information, portals, links, fields, missions, scores, COMM messages and activity, and inventory when your account has access. Player Tracker derives history from activity returned by Intel; it does not obtain other players' device GPS locations.
  • COMM messages. When you send a message, IRIS sends its text, selected channel and map-center coordinates to Intel using your signed-in session. Other players may see the message according to the service's channel rules. Reading COMM also sends requests for the selected map area.
  • Optional device location. Tapping Locate requests foreground location permission and uses the returned position to center the map. Android may provide approximate or precise location, depending on your permission choice. The app does not request background location permission. You can deny or revoke location permission and continue using other features. Map requests made after locating can reveal that area to Intel and map providers.
  • Map areas and online searches. Browsing the map sends requested areas to Intel and the selected map provider. Online address/place searches send your search text and, for a bounded search, the current map bounds to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. Portal and mission images, ornaments and artifacts load from the hosts supplied by the relevant services.
  • Local settings and drawings. Preferences, map view, drawn links/markers and some feature caches are kept in app or browser storage. These are used to restore your choices and app state. Android app backup and device-transfer backup are disabled in the app's configuration.
  • Clipboard and links. Copy actions write your selected data, such as a portal title, link or drawing, to the device clipboard. Pasting/importing uses the content you choose to provide. Opening an external link sends you to the selected service in your browser.

Services that receive network requests

Requests go directly from your device to the relevant service, rather than through an IRIS-operated proxy. The recipient receives normal connection information such as your IP address and request headers, along with the information needed for that request. Depending on the feature, recipients include:

  • Intel and your sign-in provider: authentication, session and game requests.
  • OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap and their resource hosts: map tiles, styles and related resources. OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives online search requests.
  • Image hosts: requests for portal, mission and other map imagery.
  • GitHub: the Android manual update checker fetches public build metadata only when you request a check, without Intel cookies or account details. Releases, source-code and feedback links also open GitHub. This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which receives requests to view it.
  • Google Play: if you install through Play, Google handles distribution and updates under its own terms and privacy practices.

Relevant service information includes Niantic's privacy policy, Google's privacy policy, the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy, OpenFreeMap's service information and GitHub's privacy statement. We do not control those services' retention or processing practices.

Diagnostics, support and advertising

IRIS operates no analytics, advertising or automatic crash-reporting backend. The app does not sell personal data. Native diagnostics contain technical status such as app/Android/WebView versions, page hosts, session-check results and permission status. Other diagnostic exports or screenshots may contain map or game data. Diagnostics stay on your device unless you choose to copy or send them.

If you email support or submit feedback, the maintainer receives what you send, including your email address or GitHub identity, message and any attachments. This information is used to answer requests and investigate problems. Email is handled through Gmail; GitHub issues and their attachments may be public. Review reports before sending them, and do not include passwords, cookies or session tokens.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • On your device: session data, preferences and saved drawings can persist between launches; temporary caches can be replaced during use. You can remove Android app data using system settings or by uninstalling. In a browser, clear the relevant Intel site storage/cookies and remove the extension if desired. Clearing data also removes saved local choices and drawings.
  • Signing out: Intel logout ends the Intel session while preserving local preferences. It does not delete your Ingress or sign-in-provider account. Account deletion and requests concerning data held by those services must be made through the relevant provider.
  • Support correspondence: the maintainer retains messages as needed to respond, investigate an issue and meet applicable obligations. Email the support address to request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied. We will address requests concerning information under our control; provider records and public copies may be subject to their own processes.
  • Permissions and optional features: you can revoke location permission in Android settings, leave location disabled, choose a map provider and avoid sending feedback or online searches. Local clipboard contents can be cleared through your device's clipboard controls.

Security and international processing

The app's configured service endpoints use HTTPS. Local app data is protected by the app/browser and operating system's storage controls. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Services such as Intel, authentication providers, map hosts, GitHub and Gmail may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies.

Children and policy changes

IRIS is a companion for existing Ingress users and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the maintainer, contact the support email so it can be addressed.

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes that require additional notice or consent will also be communicated through the app or its release information.
