IRIS — Ingress Intel for Android and browsers

Explore portals, plan links and follow COMM with an Intel map companion designed to keep essential information and common actions within reach on smaller screens.

Download test builds Installation instructions

Friends alpha for Android and Chrome/Firefox browser extensions. Android sign-in uses an experimental WebView workaround; Firefox builds require temporary loading unless signed. Read the installation guidance before trying a build.

Explore the map

Inspect portal details, resonators, mods and statistics. Find missions, check regional scores, and view Intel over OpenStreetMap or OpenFreeMap.

Plan your next move

Draw links and markers, inspect link ranges and inventory key counts, share portal locations, and open destinations in your preferred maps tool.

Keep activity in view

Read and send COMM, filter loaded messages, and follow player activity derived from Intel events. Interface theme and text size are independent of the base map.

IRIS means Ingress Reconnaissance & Intelligence System. It uses IITC-CE core and relevant plugins as its behavioral reference, with an independent mobile interface. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Niantic or IITC.
